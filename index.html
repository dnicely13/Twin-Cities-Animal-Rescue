Welcome to Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and neglected animals across the Twin Cities metropolitan area. We are dedicated to advocating for animal welfare, educating our community on responsible pet ownership, and matching every rescue animal with a loving, permanent family.

Our Community Impact

  • Over 1,200 animals successfully placed in loving homes last year.
  • Active foster network supporting over 150 dogs, cats, and small pets at any given time.
  • 100% spay/neuter and microchip compliance for all adopted pets.
