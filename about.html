Our Story & History

Founded in 2015 by a collective of local animal welfare advocates and veterinary professionals, Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a small grassroots foster network. Over the past decade, we have grown into one of the region's leading animal rescue organizations, operating on a commitment to transparency, compassion, and community-driven rescue work.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion First: Every decision we make prioritizes the physical and emotional well-being of the animals in our care.
  • Community Partnership: We believe animal welfare is a community effort, working hand-in-hand with volunteers, local shelters, and adopters.
  • Education & Advocacy: We advocate for responsible pet ownership, spay/neuter programs, and humane treatment legislation across the state.
