Our Programs & Services

We offer several life-saving programs designed to connect rescue animals with compassionate humans. Explore our adoption, fostering, and volunteer pathways below.

The Adoption Process

  1. Browse & Connect: View our available animals online or visit our shelter during visiting hours.
  2. Submit an Application: Fill out our adoption questionnaire online or in-person.
  3. Meet & Greet: Spend one-on-one time with your potential new pet with guidance from our adoption coordinators.
  4. Finalize Adoption: Complete the adoption fee agreement, medical history review, and take your new family member home!

Foster Care Program

Temporary foster homes save lives by freeing up shelter space and providing individualized care, socialization, and rehabilitation for animals recovering from medical procedures or adjusting to shelter life. Twin Cities Animal Rescue provides all necessary food, supplies, and veterinary care for our foster parents.

Volunteer Roles

  • Shelter Care & Enrichment: Assist with daily feeding, grooming, walking, and socialization for dogs, cats, and small animals.
  • Transport Drivers: Help transport rescue animals to veterinary appointments and adoption events.
  • Community Outreach: Represent Twin Cities Animal Rescue at adoption fairs, fundraisers, and educational workshops.
